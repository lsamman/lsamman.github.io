    Field notes, read before use
    • There is no server. Ghost messages are locked with AES-GCM in your browser, and the key is in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to a website.
    • That means anyone who has the link can read the message until it expires. Send it only to the person it is for.
    • “Burned” is remembered on the device that opened the link. Another phone or browser can still open it.
    • The expiry is checked by this app, not by a server. Someone who knows how could still unlock an old link.
    • Screenshots exist. So does a second phone pointed at the screen.
    • The timer on a shared snap is just for show. Use “Ghost link” if you want it to burn.
    • Snaps and photos never leave your device unless you share them.
